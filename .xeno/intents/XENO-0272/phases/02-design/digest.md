The term is removed rather than replaced. The replacement that looked right — keying the
exemption on the absence of a `context.lock.yaml`, which is a fact about the tree — exempts
nobody: all 495 phases in the trail carry a lock, the two pre-M0 intakes included. It would
have been a term that never fires, written to look like a safeguard, and generous in the wrong
direction later, since a phase whose lock had been deleted would have its `context_hash`
excused.

The comment keeps the reason it removed, because it is sound and a reader who finds the obvious
exemption missing deserves to know what was wrong with it. `hashShape`'s comment now names what
the two remaining terms have in common — both are facts about the tree — rather than counting
cases, which is the rule that stops somebody adding a third term from a field.

Four approvals, one per finding, because a decision is recorded against a finding and not a
phase. The reason differs between the intents by one date, since the whole of it is that
nothing could compute the value then, and it says the finding is correct rather than spurious,
because the easy reading of an approval is that the gate was wrong.

One row in the register rather than a note in the code: the next person who finds a check that
would be simpler with a declaration needs the reason it was taken out, and the four approvals
need to be findable from somewhere other than two `gate.yaml` files.

No sentence in section 12. It was the issue's first shape, the maintainer chose the second, and
the defect is gone rather than documented.

D-1 records the choice with the three shapes and the measurement behind it.
