Six alternatives, and the one worth the record is the startpoint. Beginning the sequence
at the next free number, 0122, is tighter and puts the two schemes adjacent with nothing
marking the boundary, which moves again the moment an issue reaches 122. The gap at two
hundred is the cheapest possible marker and the reason the user named it. The rest of
the design is one decision repeated: the order is a query over a recorded field, not a
property of a string.
