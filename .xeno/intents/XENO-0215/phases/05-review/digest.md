The three standing rules hold and #149 is implemented rather than reversed: Xeno ships no indexer, and the producer
is this project's own tooling behind a build tag that keeps it out of the module. A42's property gains a second
clause in the same CI step, since an index is the thing a gate would most like to consult and exactly what must not
be consulted. The reviewer's first target should be kind and container as free strings — a format with
unconstrained fields is arguably not a format, and the counter is that the constraint would be Xeno's taxonomy
imposed on four languages it does not parse. Criterion 2 is claimed as half met and a reviewer may fairly call it
unmet. The risk that outlives the intent is that provenance is self reported: a freshness check over a timestamp
the producer wrote catches the honest producer and not the broken one, which is the case that gives confidently
wrong answers.
